    It's okay to make mistakes

    发表于 2025-12-01

    At the beginning, everything seems to be simple

    Mistakes,mistakes. No one could ever say that he or she never made a mistake. Mistake is common. Every time, every place, mistakes just occurs.

    We are told to be careful, like people should never make mistakes. Just like preventing to make mistakes is as easy as drinking or eating. Simple, at least we are told so.

    But life would not be so simple

    the fact is : we can’t stop making mistakes. Just one month before(2025.11), the World Wide Web suffered from two “disasters”. First the aws shutdown, then the cloudflare. It is thought that things like “WWW” is strong and unbreakable. However we are wrong. It is so fragile. Any mistakes we made could destory it.

    Actually, could you imagine our society is built upon how many assumptions? Like, crypto things. It basicly relies on that P != NP, that means not every hard quesion have an easy way to get a solution. However, we don’t know whether it is ture or not. If someone magically find a way to solve RSA. The whole crypto world would be destoryed. https? Not safe now. TLS? same as https. At least we can use bitcoin, it is based on hash, not RSA.

    Life is so hard, what should we do

    Embrace mistakes, seriously.

    what???

    You are just talking about mistakes are disastrous. Now you need us to embrace mistakes!?

    Well, just what I said in paragraph one. Everyone makes mistakes. There is no way for us to prevent mistakes. Even robots, artificial intelligence would make mistakes. So what we need is not to prevent mistakes, but to prevent mistakes making damages.

    5 years ago, when openai hasn’t release chatgpt, we may say that spelling is important. PC are not human, they can’t tolerant your wrong mistakes. Things have changed now in 2025, you can just throw a bunch of letters ignoring grammar or spelling into chatgpt or other LLMs, and get a good solution. At past, if you input something like: “mr.beassts” into search engines like google, you would not get the right thing. But now, you can have a try, and enjoys mr.beast’s youtube videos.

    What I want to say is that we need a system that can recognice mistakes and auto correct them. Rather than a system that needs operators never made mistakes. No one is perfect, and we need tolerate that.
