Should the better players ALWAYS win?

in many compititive games , people tends to belive that if someone plays better, he or she is more likely to win. Howerver, I have to say, NO.

You may argue that if people pays more effort, they should gain more rewards ,else it would be unfair to them. But do we need a fair game that gives rewards and happineses only to 20% of the players, leaving the rest suffering from frustration and sadness?

According to the video mentioned above, lossing is twice as painfull as winning is pleasent. Let’s do a simple math here:
only who holds 66% or more win rate can gain happiness from playing a game. Also let’s say player’s win rate follows a normal distribution, with average at 50% and standard deviation at 10%. That means only 16% of the players can gain happiness from playing the game, while the rest 84% are suffering from sadness. That really sucks.

FAIR NOT EQUALS TO FUN

In some games, like chess , both players face the same situation, that is what we call a symmetric games. Howerver, does a symmetic game can let both players have fun? Not actually. Imagine that just because a mistake you made 5 minutes after the game started, and you have to sufer for 10 or more minutes to find a chance to comeback, or jus t lose the game directly. That is awful.

In chess, the first player(usally white)has a significant advatage, they can attack first, that would make the case even worth. You didn’t make any mistake, but you still lose the game, that sucks.

SOLUTION?

how can we fix that? (I’ll not talk about solutions metioned in the video, go watch it if you are interested) In chess, it offers a oppotunity to the losing player to find a way to draw the game. That is a good idea, we do not need to give fair oppotunities to both players. There is no one who is winning the game want to paly a draw, but not same to the losing one. We can give some oppotunities with lower expectations but with higher distribution, so the losing player can have a chance to win or draw the game. like in some rougelike games(although they are pve not pvp), there are some items that offers random effects, most of the time, they just like dirt, but when you are in the mud too, they are greatlt helpful.

ADVANTAGE OF MISTAKES

Besides that, we can also give some advantages to the players who make mistakes, like in Street Fighter 6, players with low HP can use super move knows as “CA” to turn the tide of the game. Also in games like Monster Hunter series(also not pvp),players sometimes get benifits from gettinh hit, like getting the power of life steal or deals more damage. That can make making mistakes less horrible, and gives more chances to the losing ones, adding more dramatic moments to the game.

CONCLUSION

So, does well-play means win? NO, or let’s say, we should not let it always means win. we are supposed to having funs in games, not suffering or geting anxious. Imagine you are 1 40 yeasr old man ,ending boring woek after a long day ,finally get home from a traffic jam. “It’s ten o’clock, I will have a quick a game to finsh the f-word day”, and you get totally crushed bt a 12 years old teens who has no means of politeness. that would be a totally nightmare. Games should be fun, not any means of ability test.
