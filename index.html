Should the better players ALWAYS win?

in many compititive games , people tends to belive that if someone plays better, he or she is more likely to win. Howerver, I have to say, NO.

You may argue that if people pays more effort, they should gain more rewards ,else it would be unfair to them. But do we need a fair game that gives rewards and happineses only to 20% of the players, leaving the rest suffering from frustration and sadness?

According to the video mentioned above, lossing is twice as painfull as winning is pleasent. Let’s do a simple math here:
only who holds 66% or more win rate can gain happiness from playing a game. Also let’s say player’s win rate follows a normal distribution, with average at 50% and standard deviation at 10%. That means only 16% of the players can gain happiness from playing the game, while the rest 84% are suffering from sadness. That really sucks.

FAIR NOT EQUALS TO FUN

In some games, like chess , both players face the same situation, that is what we call a symmetric games. Howerver, does a symmetic game can let both players have fun? Not actually. Imagine that just because a mistake you made 5 minutes after the game started, and you have to sufer for 10 or more minutes to find a chance to comeback, or jus t lose the game directly. That is awful.

In chess, the first player(usally white)has a significant advatage, they can attack first, that would make the case even worth. You didn’t make any mistake, but you still lose the game, that sucks.

SOLUTION?

how can we fix that? (I’ll not talk about solutions metioned in the video, go watch it if you are interested) In chess, it offers a oppotunity to the losing player to find a way to draw the game. That is a good idea, we do not need to give fair oppotunities to both players. There is no one who is winning the game want to paly a draw, but not same to the losing one. We can give some oppotunities with lower expectations but with higher distribution, so the losing player can have a chance to win or draw the game. like in some rougelike games(although they are pve not pvp), there are some items that offers random effects, most of the time, they just like dirt, but when you are in the mud too, they are greatlt helpful.

ADVANTAGE OF MISTAKES

Besides that, we can also give some advantages to the players who make mistakes, like in Street Fighter 6, players with low HP can use super move knows as “CA” to turn the tide of the game. Also in games like Monster Hunter series(also not pvp),players sometimes get benifits from gettinh hit, like getting the power of life steal or deals more damage. That can make making mistakes less horrible, and gives more chances to the losing ones, adding more dramatic moments to the game.

CONCLUSION

So, does well-play means win? NO, or let’s say, we should not let it always means win. we are supposed to having funs in games, not suffering or geting anxious. Imagine you are 1 40 yeasr old man ,ending boring woek after a long day ,finally get home from a traffic jam. “It’s ten o’clock, I will have a quick a game to finsh the f-word day”, and you get totally crushed bt a 12 years old teens who has no means of politeness. that would be a totally nightmare. Games should be fun, not any means of ability test.

first,regular language

what is regular language?

in general,regular language consists five elements:

  1. an alphbet(meaning a finite set of symbols)
  2. “” (meaning Concatenation)(you don’t need to write it explicitly)
  3. “|” (meaning union)(or)
  4. “*” (meaning kleene star)
  5. “()”(meaning group)

for example,here is a sentence in RE:

(aa)b(aa)

which means:
a sentence that starts with n number of “aa”, where n could be 0, followed by a “b”, and ends with m number of “aa”, where m could be 0.

very easy,right?

next, NFA

NFA means Non-deterministic Finite Automaton.
we don’t care about what does it mean now.
let’s just introduce “nodes” and “edges” to express RE, ‘cause it’s more visualized.

so, we will define “nodes” first:

  1. a node is a representation of a state in the automation.

oh please wait!

what is state? and what is automation?

you can just think automation is a machine that have different states, in every state, the machine can do sepecific things.

let’s say: John is an automation, he has three states: “hungry”, “thirsty” and “idle”.

when John is in “idle” state, as time passed by ,he could be thirty or hungry, either of them would make him change his state.

when John is in “hungry” state, he would eat something and change his state to “idle”.

when John is in “thirsty” state, he would drink something and change his state to “idle”.

so, in this example, “hungry”, “thirsty” and “idle” are all states of John(the automation).

anyway, back to nodes

  1. a node is a representation of a state in the automation.
  2. nodes have types:
    • one and only one: start node, where the whole automation starts. in RE, it’s the beginning of the sentence.
    • zero or more: middles nodes, where the automation mostly works with the input.
    • at least one: accept nodes, where the automation ends. you may wonder why RE only have one end, but we have more here, we will discuss it later.
  3. nodes are connected by edges.

edges

  1. edges are connections between nodes.
  2. edges have one and only one direction, from one node to another.
  3. egdes have packages, which indecates what kind of input can make the automation go through this edge.
    in our John-automation example, time ,eating and drinking are all packages on edges. also noting that edges can have none package, meaning that the automation can go through this edge without any input.

At the beginning, everything seems to be simple

Mistakes,mistakes. No one could ever say that he or she never made a mistake. Mistake is common. Every time, every place, mistakes just occurs.

Life is so hard, what should we do

Embrace mistakes, seriously.

what???

You are just talking about mistakes are disastrous. Now you need us to embrace mistakes!?

Well, just what I said in paragraph one. Everyone makes mistakes. There is no way for us to prevent mistakes. Even robots, artificial intelligence would make mistakes. So what we need is not to prevent mistakes, but to prevent mistakes making damages.

5 years ago, when openai hasn’t release chatgpt, we may say that spelling is important. PC are not human, they can’t tolerant your wrong mistakes. What I want to say is that we need a system that can recognice mistakes and auto correct them. Rather than a system that needs operators never made mistakes. No one is perfect, and we need tolerate that.

the MOO

发表于 2025-03-31

我们将创造一种全新的语言-MOO

语言小课堂

对于自然语言来说,最常见的构成是SVO(中/英文),或者SOV(日文),甚至OSV(还是日文)
它们,都太复杂了

  • 对于解析来说
    于是我们创造了MOO

MOO是什么?

M stands for Method && O stands for Object
即方法+对象1+对象2
任何一个句子,都有M+O+O构成
这是一种波兰式语法,显然,更加方便解析

  • 只要入栈就好了吗
    那么,什么是方法,而什么是对象?
    方法,很简单,只是一个二元谓词,返回一个对象或布尔值
  • 让我们先把返回值放一边
    对象,相反,很复杂,让我们用递归来定义
  • 一个名词,是对象
  • 一个布尔值,是对象
  • 一个代词,也是对象
  • 一个句子(M+O+O),是对象
    这就是为什么M需要一个返回值

简单的MOO示例

中文: 我吃饭
MOO: 吃我饭

  • 二元谓词后面所接对象的顺序,根据二元谓词本身的定义而定
    中文:小明和我踢足球
    MOO:踢(和(小明,我),足球)
  • 书写复杂MOO句子时最好加括号(不加也不会有歧义,这点以后再说),读就不需要了
    中文:我和小明踢球,导致我受伤
    MOO:导致(踢(和(小明,我),球),受(我,伤))

为什么不会有歧义

对于任意的M,都必须接收两个参数
不存在多参数情况
任何的MOO组合,都代表一个封闭的语句,即可化为O,递归处理,绝无多义性可能

语义拓展

然而自然语言不只有SVO,还有adj.,adv.等等
在MOO语法体系下,似乎无法实现这些成分
这时就需要拓展语法

何为拓展

众所周知,人类的自然语言都属于一个有限域,我们对其拓展,即是对有限域进行拓展,只需加入有限个元素,即可让我们的MOO语法得以表达其余成分

如何拓展

目前来看,似乎只需拓展2次,加入2个”保留字”即可,暂定为”de”,”ed”
当我们需要对一个O进行修饰,只需使用de(修饰词,修饰对象)即可
如:
中文:这是一朵漂亮的花
MOO:是(这,de(漂亮,一朵花))
English: This is a cute dog.
MOO: is(this,de(cute,a dog))
当我们需要对一个O进行限定,我们使用ed,ed(限定词,限定对象)
如:
中文:在所有人之中,你是最棒的
MOO:de(最棒的,ed(所有人,你))
English:you are the worst in class
MOO: de(worst,ed(class,you))
当我们需要对一个M进行修饰,我们使用de(修饰词,修饰对象)
你可能有疑问,不是M吗?怎么成为修饰对象
看一下例子,你就能明白
中文:我快速地吃饭
MOO:de(快速,吃(我,饭))
English:I run slowly.
MOO:de(slowly,run(I,NULL))

  • 这里出现了自然语言中的单元谓词,只需填NULL即可
    在MOO语言中,不可能出现单独的M,任何的M都会被包裹成一个O,所有可以用de
    对于限定M,同理,我们使用ed
    如:
    中文:五乘(在模10下)三等于5
    MOO:等于(5,ed(模10,乘(3,5)))
    English:in English,speaking needs practice
    MOO: need(ed(English,speak(NULL,NULL)),practice)
  • 对于部分0元谓词,我们直接填NULL即可
    至此,我们实现了MOO语言(至少是陈述句方面)

再次拓展

仅仅表述陈述句还不够,我们还需要让MOO语言能表达疑问句
这时我们需要再拓展一次(是的,只要一次)
我们再加入一个元素 “?”
我们知道,一个MOO语句的返回值要么是对象,要么是布尔值,显然,返回值是对象的MOO语句,无法成为疑问句
而对于返回值是布尔值的MOO语句,就可以被改造成疑问句

一般疑问句

一个MOO语句,若带有?元素,即代表其为疑问句,即请求对方做出回答,回答即是MOO语言为真时,?的一个解
让我们看一个例子:
中文:
你吃饭了吗?
是的
MOO:
是(吃(你,饭),?)
isTure(1,NULL)
相当简单,且回答不仅限此,任何恒真/恒假语句均可作为一般疑问句的解

其他疑问句

然而,我们有些时候,不只询问真假,还需询问一个或多个对象
也很简单
如:
中文:
你的内裤是什么颜色?
内裤是白色.
MOO:
是(de(你,内裤),ed(?,颜色))
是(内裤,白色)

总结

我去,MOO,伟大,来源于密码学课上的胡思乱想,花了40分钟完善,目前来说,还算ok吧(大概)

From-none-to-BACKUP

发表于 2025-02-23

请配合第二片NAS教程食用

如何使你的数据更安全

记从0开始在服务器上部署定时备份程序

备份类型

从理论上来讲,我们希望我们的备份能起到效果,即可以通过备份恢复我们的文档,一个自然的想法诞生了:每次我修改文件后,创建一个该文件夹的备份不就行了?这就是所谓的完整备份.但是,聪明的你肯定想到了,我可以在每次变动时,只对被改变的文件做一次备份不就行了?这就是所谓的增量备份.

完整备份

完整备份的好处是简单,要恢复哪天的备份直接把当天的备份解压即可
代价是更大的存储占用
首先,我们先写一个自动备份脚本:
sudo nano full_back.sh
输入:
#!/bin/bash
SOURCE_DIR=”/path/to/source”
BACKUP_DIR=”/path/to/backup”
DATE=$(date +”%Y-%m-%d_%H-%M-%S”)
BACKUP_FILE=”$BACKUP_DIR/backup_$DATE.tar.gz”
tar -czf “$BACKUP_FILE” “$SOURCE_DIR”
echo “Backup completed at $(date) -> $BACKUP_FILE” >> /home/pi/backup.log
即可

  • 注:请将SOURCE_DIR和BACKUP_DIR改成实际路径
    接着,我们要给这个文件权限:
    chmod +x /home/pi/full_backup.sh
    然后,我们利用cron实现定期执行:
    0 1 * * * /home/pi/full_backup.sh #凌晨1点运行
    完成
    当你需要恢复备份时,解压对应备份即可

增量备份

增量备份,相对的更加复杂,但也更加小巧
首先,我们需要rsync(树莓派自带,没有自行安装)
然后,我们需要一个备份脚本:
sudo nano backup.sh
输入:
#!/bin/bash
SOURCE_DIR=”/path/to/source”
BACKUP_DIR=”/path/to/backup”
rsync -av –delete “$SOURCE_DIR” “$BACKUP_DIR”
echo “Backup completed at $(date)” >> /home/pi/backup.log
即可

  • 同样的,请换成实际路径
    赋予执行权限:
    chmod +x /home/pi/backup.sh
    使用cron自动运行:
    0 2 * * * /home/pi/backup.sh
    即可
    当你需要恢复备份时,动动你的大脑(非贬义)将待恢复的文件分解为多个增量备份的并集,再逐一解压即可.

总结

如何闲着没事干记录生活

记从0开始利用hexo和GitHub pages部署个人博客

GitHub注册

要想在GitHub Pages上部署个人博客,至少你得先有一个GitHub账号,这部分不在赘述,相信你的能力

Github pages 申请

并非所有GitHub账号都自带Github Pages仓库.点击右上角+号,选择New repository创建新仓库
设置仓库信息为:
仓库名:必须是：你的GitHub用户名.github.io（比如你的 GitHub 用户名是 xiaoming，那仓库名就是 xiaoming.github.io）
选择 Public
不要勾选Readme.md,保持为一个空仓库
点击Create repository创建仓库

本地安装依赖

Hexo需要Node.js与Git,分别下载之,Node.js选择LTS版本

Hexo安装

在你的电脑上开辟一块区域,用来存放你的博客

  • 为方便叙述,这里以 D:\Blog为例
    打开powershell
    mkdir D:\Blog #创建文件夹
    cd D:\Blog #进入文件夹
    npm install -g hexo-cli #安装hexo
    hexo init #hexo初始化
    npm install #安装依赖

本地运行博客

在powershell下:
hexo clean #清除之前缓存
hexo g #生成网页
hexo s #在本地运行
然后访问http://localhost:4000
即可看到默认的博客页面

安装Github Pages插件

hexo需要一个插件,来讲网站部署到Github Pages上
在powershell中:
npm install hexo-deployer-git –save

配置_config.yml

最重要的是修改deploy部分,以将网站部署到GitHub Pages上
修改为:
deploy:
type: git
repo: git@github.com:你的GitHub用户名/你的GitHub用户名.github.io.git
branch: main
其余的有注释,只需中学英语水平即可,按自己喜好修改即可
然后记得保存

部署到Github Pages

在powershell中:
hexo clean
hexo g
hexo d #部署你的博客

  • 第一次使用需进行身份验证,需要ssh key,我们等下讲怎么做

访问你的博客

在https://你的GitHub用户名.github.io即可看到你的博客

身份验证

ssh key生成

先生成ssh key:
ssh-keygen -t rsa -b 4096 -C “你的GitHub邮箱”
再添加你的ssh key到GitHub上:
复制 ~/.ssh/id_rsa.pub 的内容
打开 GitHub SSH 设置，点击 New SSH Key，粘贴进去
重新部署即可

怎么写文章

在你的博客文件夹下找到此目录:source/_posts/
里面存放你的博客(md格式即可)
每次更改需重新部署一次
